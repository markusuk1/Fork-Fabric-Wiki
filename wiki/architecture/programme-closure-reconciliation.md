## Reconciliation rule

Do not convert a future-looking matrix statement into an implied commitment.
First research prior art, assign a stable task, define exact ownership and
acceptance criteria, then change its status. Conversely, when a downstream task
is completed, update upstream wiki boundary prose so it describes the local
component boundary without calling the delivered consumer “future.”
