## Overview

The village currently contains several real end-to-end vertical proofs, but a
proof of one selected path is not the same as a general production system.
Future work is governed by explicit capability contracts covering the actual
player flow, physical authority, durable state, failure recovery, persistence
and observable evidence.

The selected architecture remains **Combine**: OpenMW 0.51/API 129 owns
frame-time sensing, AI, game time, interface, inventory, crime and spatial
sound; Fork build 36 owns durable cognitive, social, economic and work state;
the companion transports typed/versioned events, intentions and receipts.

## Honest as-built boundary

Delivered foundations include the clean world shell, exact ten-actor village,
native merchant, real inventory/crime reconciliation, holder-specific
knowledge, deterministic profile reactions, persistent interaction UI and a
working production companion. These are reusable foundations.

The original audit found the following prototype-only or absent boundaries;
each bullet now states its post-programme disposition:

- generalized attack, death, native crime, stance and embodied speech now enter
  one observer-specific perception/appraisal gateway; staged murder and
  appearance paths are retained only as explicit regression fixtures after
  migration to the generalized gateway;
- generalized social transmission is now embodied, privacy/range-aware,
  overhearable, trust-bearing and bounded to provenance-preserving retellings;
  the legacy timer path remains only as an explicit regression fixture;
- explainable high-level goal/intention selection is generalized and delivered;
  embodied social consequence propagation is generalized and delivered;
- the player journal gap is closed: five source kinds, corrections, filters,
  native Journal access and revision-checked topic selection are delivered;
- person-to-person quantity, player-selected price, counter-offer and
  compensated physical settlement are delivered;
- the first commerce good is now produced as a finite provenance lot, delivered
  by an embodied carrier and reconciled through purchase/theft into scarcity;
  broader goods, producers and demand remain later expansion;
- deterministic dialogue, ordered context history and typed actions are
  delivered;
- bounded conversation/terminal-repair and proactive observation-range
  cognition leases plus provider-neutral spatial voice are delivered;
- native five-screen character creation, opaque player/save lineage,
  migration/version gates, exact save/process/Fork restart and promoted v183
  desktop resume are delivered by PLAYER-001.
- household/work membership, capacity-aware occupancy, exact active/inactive
  embodiment and inactive-only reconciliation are delivered by POP-002 with
  save/load, Fork restart, companion crash/reconnect and day/night evidence.

## Delivery order

The core dependency path is bridge/profile/routines, then generalized
perception/memory/decision, then social/journal/reputation, then economy, and
finally bounded LLM/voice. QA-001 now closes integrated save/load,
cell-transition, outage and bounded long-run qualification with 10/10 gates,
zero final engine/Lua/dead-letter errors and unchanged production state.

Bridge, profile, routines, perception, memory, decisions, embodied social flow,
journal, negotiated commerce, the first causal supply chain, bounded
deterministic contextual conversation and bounded external cognition leases are
delivered. LLM-002 now closes proactive observation-range “interact or wait”
assessment; VOICE-001 and QA-001 now close the remaining dependency-ordered
voice and integrated-qualification commitments.

`COMMERCE-001` is a safe immediate exception because the existing interaction
and receipt boundary already isolates it. Completing quantity, asking-price and
counter-offer mechanics repairs an exposed player-facing gap without creating
a second authority or blocking the foundational work.

## Evidence standard

No feature is accepted from seeded rows, test-only flags or debug logs alone.
Every applicable task must prove static rejection, transactional reducer state,
the production companion, real OpenMW physical behaviour, stale/offline safety,
persistence across save/load or cell transitions, error/performance limits, and
visual or audio evidence for player-facing behaviour.
